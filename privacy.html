Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

What we collect

Account details: your email address or phone number, and a password (stored by our authentication provider, not in readable form).

Profile details you add: name, birthday (we store your age from it), sex, relationship status, what you are looking for, bio, location text, icebreaker answers, and photos.

Activity you create: messages, photos and GIFs you send in direct chats, group chat messages, swipes, matches, blocks and reports, and activities you add or RSVP to.

Device details for notifications: a push notification token for each device where you allow notifications.

What we do not collect

We do not track your location. The location field is text you type yourself.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

How we use it

To run the app: showing your profile to other passengers on your cruise, matching, and delivering messages.

To send notifications you have not turned off in Settings (new matches, messages, and activity updates).

To keep the community safe: handling blocks and reports.

Who we share it with

Supabase: hosts our database, sign-in, and photo storage.

Expo: delivers push notifications to your device.

GIPHY: when you search for a GIF, your search text is sent to GIPHY to find results.

Other passengers see the profile information you choose to show. You can hide your age, location, or whole profile in Settings.

We may disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

Your data stays until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your profile, matches, messages, swipes, blocks, and group memberships.

Your choices

Edit your profile at any time.

Turn notifications on or off, and hide your profile, age, or location, in Settings.

Delete your account in Settings. This cannot be undone.

Children

Ship'D is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor is using the app, contact us and we will remove the account.
